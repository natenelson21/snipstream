# SnipStream

SnipStream is a personal desktop app that turns its owner's stream highlight clips into short vertical videos and posts them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is used by one creator, its owner, and is not offered to the public.

Nothing is posted automatically. The owner reviews each clip and presses Publish. Before a TikTok post is sent, SnipStream shows the TikTok account it will post to, the caption, who can view the video, the comment, duet and stitch settings, and any commercial content disclosure.
