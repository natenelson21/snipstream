# SnipStream Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

SnipStream is a personal desktop app used by its owner to post their own stream highlight clips to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what it accesses and how that information is handled.

## Information SnipStream accesses

From TikTok, with your permission: your basic profile information (account ID, display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, and permission to publish videos to your account through the video.publish scope. Before each post SnipStream also reads your current posting options, such as the privacy levels and interaction settings available to your account.

From YouTube, with your permission: permission to upload videos to your channel and to read statistics such as views, likes and comments for videos SnipStream posted.

SnipStream also stores the clips it creates and the captions, titles and settings you choose for each post.

## How it is used

This information is used only to post the clips you choose, with the settings you choose, to your own accounts, and to show you whether each post succeeded and how it is performing.

## Storage and security

Sign-in tokens, account IDs, clips and post records are kept in the owner's private database and storage, and in a configuration file on the owner's computer. They are not exposed publicly or to the app's interface.

## Sharing

SnipStream does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and does not use it for advertising or analytics. Videos you publish are sent only to the platform you choose.

## Retention and deletion

Sign-in tokens are kept only while your account is connected. You can disconnect SnipStream at any time from the app permissions section of your TikTok or Google account settings. Saved tokens and records can be deleted from the owner's database on request.

## Children

SnipStream is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
